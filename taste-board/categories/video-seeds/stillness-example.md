## What the detail modal teaches

The “Stillness” modal shows that a useful reference-library entry can preserve more than a screenshot:

1. A human-readable title.
2. A large source image.
3. A short interpretation of the design’s visual idea.
4. Compact visual-vocabulary chips. Visible examples include “voxel-rendered landscape,” “pixel mountains and river,” “serif headline with green emphasis,” “clean white ground,” “muted blue-coral-green palette,” and “soft product buttons.”
5. A longer reusable brief or prompt block.
6. Explicit actions to copy the brief and copy the image prompt.
7. Small classification metadata near the title.

The transcript says the detail view explains what a design is and means, supplies associated vocabulary/keywords, and lets the user copy an image prompt for reuse in a hero image. This supports an entry structure that connects evidence, interpretation, vocabulary, and downstream making.
