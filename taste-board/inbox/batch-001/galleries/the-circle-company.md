## What it is and does

The Circle Company is a product studio positioning AI-assisted workflows as a way to move from brief to live product in weeks rather than months. The page combines a direct speed promise with project work, an agency philosophy statement, service categories, and a project-start CTA.

## Aesthetic family

High-contrast tech studio; dark editorial brutalism; Web3-influenced product agency.

## Visual vocabulary

- Black cinematic canvas
- Blurred magenta light field
- Oversized stacked grotesk headline
- White-and-hot-pink contrast
- Compact mint identity mark
- Hairline section dividers
- Wide project-image strips
- Numbered service columns

## Composition and hierarchy

The hero leaves most of the upper viewport dark and atmospheric, then anchors the promise along the bottom in an asymmetrical two-column lockup: an enormous statement on the left and compact explanatory copy plus CTA on the right. A project strip follows, then a large manifesto, vertical service columns, and a closing oversized CTA. The persistent logo and hamburger keep navigation peripheral while typography does the primary work.

## Typography

A bold modern grotesk carries the main statements in very large, tightly spaced lines. Regular-weight sans serif supports descriptions and project metadata, while small uppercase labels and numerals create a quiet technical layer. Pink emphasis is applied to whole clauses, turning color into semantic pacing rather than decoration.

## Color, texture, and imagery

Near-black is the dominant field. Hot red-pink supplies the main accent, with a soft mint logo and occasional gray secondary text. A blurred magenta-to-brown light wash gives the hero and footer depth. Project imagery appears in wide editorial crops, often subdued enough to preserve the site's dark tonal continuity.

## What to borrow

Borrow the confidence of placing one blunt value proposition at extreme scale, then supporting it with a much smaller operational explanation. Use accent color to stress the result or time contrast, maintain persistent peripheral navigation, and let a blurred light field soften an otherwise severe black-and-white system.

## Do not copy

Do not reuse The Circle Company identity, “We Build & Ship,” “Weeks, Not Months,” the exact magenta treatment, client images, service list, or circular mint logo. Avoid making unsupported speed claims merely because the visual language feels fast.
