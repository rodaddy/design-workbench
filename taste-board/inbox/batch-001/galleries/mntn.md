## What it is and does

MNTN is a long-form hiking-guide landing page concept distributed as a free Figma template. It opens with an immersive mountain hero, then organizes beginner guidance into three numbered editorial sections covering experience level, gear, and route planning.

## Aesthetic family

Cinematic outdoor editorial; premium adventure journal; dark scenic storytelling.

## Visual vocabulary

- Full-bleed mountain photography
- Deep blue-black content field
- Oversized high-contrast serif headlines
- Mustard-gold eyebrow labels
- Monumental translucent numerals
- Alternating image-and-copy modules
- Vertical edge navigation
- Expansive negative space

## Composition and hierarchy

The hero fills the viewport with layered landscape photography and a centered serif statement, while navigation, social links, and a three-step progress rail sit at the edges. Below, three large numbered chapters alternate copy and photography across a generous two-column grid. The faint `01`, `02`, and `03` numerals anchor each section without displacing the reading hierarchy. The footer resolves into three quiet columns.

## Typography

Large, elegant display serif type carries the emotional statements and chapter titles. A compact geometric sans serif handles navigation, labels, body copy, and links. Uppercase gold eyebrow labels use wide tracking, small rules, and restrained scale to provide editorial wayfinding.

## Color, texture, and imagery

The palette is built from desaturated alpine greens, cloud gray, cool blue, and a nearly black navy background. Warm mustard accents punctuate headings and links. Photography is atmospheric and human-scaled: solitary hikers, distant ridgelines, fog, and a handheld compass. The dark field and washed scenery make the page feel archival rather than sports-retail glossy.

## Interaction and motion

The static composition implies anchor-based chapter navigation through the right-side `01–03` progress rail, a downward-scroll prompt, and arrowed “read more” links. Motion was not verified. If implemented, slow image parallax, restrained crossfades, and progress-rail state changes would fit the evidence; energetic scroll-jacking would not.

## What to borrow

Borrow the pairing of one immersive scenic opener with a disciplined numbered article sequence. Use edge-positioned utilities to keep the center cinematic, and let oversized background numerals create continuity across otherwise spacious alternating modules.

## Do not copy

Do not reuse the MNTN name, hiking copy, exact mountain photographs, `01–03` chapter wording, or the distinctive “Be Prepared For The Mountains And Beyond!” hero line. Avoid reproducing the precise screenshot as a template clone.

## Copy-ready design brief

Create a cinematic editorial landing page that opens on one full-viewport environmental photograph with a large, centered serif promise. Keep functional navigation and progress controls at the outer edges so the image and message remain dominant. Transition into a deep blue-black long-form canvas containing three generously spaced chapters; alternate the copy and image columns, place a huge low-contrast chapter number behind each heading, and use small uppercase gold labels as wayfinding. Pair an expressive high-contrast serif with a compact geometric sans serif. Use desaturated, atmospheric photography and minimal warm accents. Motion should be slow and spatial—subtle parallax, fades, and chapter-state changes—so the experience feels like a premium field journal without borrowing MNTN's identity, copy, imagery, or exact layout.
