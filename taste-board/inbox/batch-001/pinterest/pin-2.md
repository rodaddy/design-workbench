## What it is and does

A real-estate marketing landing page that helps visitors discover properties and understand the service’s value. The video showcases a dark, image-led hero followed by bright content sections, property information, an accordion-style value proposition, contact content, and a branded end card.

## Aesthetic family

Contemporary real-estate editorial, architectural minimalism, and polished startup landing page. The design balances luxury photography with approachable blue-and-orange product accents.

## Visual vocabulary

- Black split hero
- Arched image mask
- Oversized stacked headline
- Floating search field
- Cobalt action buttons
- Orange punctuation accents
- Airy white sections
- Rounded architectural photography

## Composition and hierarchy

The opening uses an asymmetric split: copy and search on the left, a tall architectural photograph clipped by a large arch on the right. Navigation is sparse and horizontal, with a cobalt contact button acting as the terminal accent. The building image breaks the hero’s lower edge, creating depth across the black-to-white transition. Deeper content reverses the density: a large arched photo sits left while a compact eyebrow, heading, paragraph, and vertically stacked accordion items sit right. Generous margins keep both compositions calm.

## Typography

A rounded geometric sans serif appears throughout. The hero headline is heavy, large, and stacked into short lines; section headings are smaller but still strongly weighted. Body copy and navigation are light and compact. Occasional orange dots behave like typographic punctuation, while blue is used for small functional markers and controls.

## Color, texture, and imagery

The hero relies on near-black, white, cobalt blue, and a warm orange accent. Subsequent sections move to a white canvas with dark navy type and faint cool-gray dividers and shadows. Architectural photography supplies texture: glass, brick, timber, evening light, and broad sky. Large rounded and arched masks soften the rectilinear buildings.

## Interaction and motion

The video presents the site as a smooth vertical journey through hero, listings/value content, contact, and logo end card. The visible value list uses accordion affordances, and the hero includes a prominent search field. The final cobalt screen isolates a white house-shaped mark. The recording supports a polished scroll showcase, but exact hover, search, accordion, and transition mechanics are not verifiable.

## What to borrow

Pair a dark, high-impact hero with bright, spacious detail sections. Use an architectural mask as the dominant shape and let the image cross a section boundary to create depth. Keep the palette disciplined: one trustworthy cool action color and one warm punctuation color. On information-heavy sections, pair one large photograph with a short vertical list rather than filling the page with equal-weight cards.

## Do not copy

Do not reproduce the Homyz name or house mark, the exact headline, building photos, arched hero arrangement, search control, copy, or value-list wording. Do not lift the specific blue/orange combination without adapting it to the new brand’s own contrast and identity system.

## Copy-ready design brief

Create a refined property-discovery landing page with an editorial architecture feel. Open with a near-black asymmetric hero: a short, bold stacked promise and simple search action on one side, and a large building photograph shaped by a softened architectural curve on the other. Let the image overlap the transition into an airy white section. Use a rounded geometric sans, deep navy text, one confident cool-blue action color, and tiny warm accents for personality. Continue with spacious two-column sections that pair one strong photograph with concise benefits or expandable details. Motion should feel like a smooth guided tour, ending on a minimal brand-color field. Preserve the source’s clarity, warmth, and premium restraint without copying its name, house icon, photography, exact arch, or wording.
