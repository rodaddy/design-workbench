## What it is and does

A portfolio and lead-generation site for a design studio specializing in AI companies. It introduces the studio, presents a grid of selected client work, layers in testimonials and mission language, and ends with a direct booking call to action.

## Aesthetic family

Precision minimalism; premium AI studio; editorial portfolio grid; soft-futurist product showcase.

## Visual vocabulary

- High-key white canvas
- Oversized display wordmark
- Compact utilitarian navigation
- Modular square project tiles
- Tight monochrome typography
- Liquid chrome orb
- Soft blur-and-opacity reveals
- Sparse blue interaction accents

## Composition and hierarchy

The page establishes a quiet utility layer with a fixed 64-pixel navigation bar, then gives the hero abundant vertical space and a centered studio statement. “Selected works” shifts into a dense three-column grid of square, rounded project tiles, creating a deliberate contrast between the airy introduction and image-led portfolio density. Testimonials use numbered, repeated editorial blocks, while the closing section returns to a singular oversized invitation and pill-shaped booking action.

## Typography

Geist handles compact navigation, labels, testimonial copy, and body text with tight negative tracking. A lighter custom Funnel Display face is reserved for the largest expressive lines, including the closing call to action. The combination feels contemporary and controlled: small functional type around large, light display statements rather than a hierarchy built from many weights.

## Color, texture, and imagery

White and near-black dominate, with pale gray project-card grounds and occasional electric blue used as a restrained interactive cue. Imagery carries most of the color. The inspected motion asset is a translucent, reflective chrome-like orb on white; its liquid interior deforms between frames while the outer silhouette stays soft and circular. Blur, low opacity, brightness adjustments, and glossy refraction provide texture without adding visual clutter.

## Interaction and motion

Motion is material to the identity: the source contains 16 `<video>` elements and nine direct MP4 URLs, plus CSS states that reveal text through opacity, vertical translation, and blur. The inspected eight-second asset continuously morphs a liquid metallic form inside a translucent sphere. Project media appears framed as draggable or animated showcases, and the page uses scroll-oriented text reveals and restrained CTA transitions. No GIF, WebM, `og:video`, or `<canvas>` was found; the available direct motion was MP4.

## What to borrow

Borrow the alternation between spacious editorial statements and compact visual grids; the strict monochrome UI around colorful work; the use of one tactile, looping abstract object as a memorable AI-era signature; and the separation of functional small type from expressive display typography. Keep motion ambient, seamless, and materially tied to the visual system.

## Do not copy

Do not reuse the ForAI name, “Design For AI” wording, client roster, project thumbnails, testimonial copy, exact liquid orb asset, or the precise three-column case-study arrangement. Avoid generic chrome-fluid decoration unless a new material metaphor is connected to the product’s own story.

## Copy-ready design brief

Create a bright, premium studio site with a disciplined editorial rhythm: a compact fixed utility nav, an unusually spacious centered introduction, then a dense grid of square work tiles. Use a neutral grotesk for small functional copy and a light, oversized display face for only the most important statements. Keep the structural palette white, near-black, and pale gray; let portfolio imagery supply color, with one electric accent reserved for interactive cues. Introduce a bespoke seamless loop of a translucent material form that slowly deforms while preserving a calm silhouette. Reveal supporting text through subtle blur, opacity, and short vertical movement. The result should feel precise, quietly futuristic, and craft-led, matching the source’s confidence without borrowing its identity, clients, copy, or chrome-orb execution.
